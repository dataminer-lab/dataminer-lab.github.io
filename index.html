DATA VISUALIZATION · DATA MINING

VIS Group Data Visualization and Data Mining Group

Guided by our faculty advisor, we study, exchange ideas, and conduct research in data visualization and data mining. Through collaborative exploration, we learn to understand data, analyze problems, and communicate our findings.

Members share knowledge through literature reviews, group discussions, and hands-on technical practice. We also use daily and weekly reports to track our research progress. More resources and communication tools are available in the group workspace.

Data Visualization

We study methods for transforming complex data into clear, accurate, and easy-to-understand visual representations that support data exploration, analysis, and communication.

Data Mining

Learn how to discover valuable patterns and regularities from data, and explore methods including data preprocessing, feature analysis, modeling and result interpretation.

Combine interactive visualization with data analysis methods, to help users discover patterns, interpret results and form new insights during the analysis process.
